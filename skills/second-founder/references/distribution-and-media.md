# Distribution, launch calendar and media

Use for the rolling 30-day plan, daily brand content, selected personal-X amplification, images and video under the saved content policy. Read the project profile, facts, account map, shared founder calendar, content inventory and delivery ledger before preparing posts. The starting preset is music-free; owners can configure their own policy during onboarding.

## Scope and account identity

- Installing this skill does not authorize publishing. Prepare local drafts until the current owner grants publication to the project's exact mapped destinations. Once that standing scope and the content gates pass, publish without per-post confirmation. Do not import another project's or founder's grants.
- Brand access and permissions never transfer across projects. Listing an existing account does not establish that it belongs to this project.
- Discover the project's actual provider and current capabilities. Verify the connection and supported posting formats rather than guessing from a product name or installed executable.
- Onboard immutable integration/account IDs, platform, live handle, intended brand, permitted formats and existing authorization. Store credential references only; never secret values.
- Revalidate destination identity before submission and after account/configuration changes. A mismatch blocks that destination and produces an actionable repair item.
- Use project accounts first. Amplify selected useful milestones or lessons on personal X only within its explicitly saved shared-account scope and available calendar allocation.
- Account creation, direct outreach, paid promotion and spend are separate capabilities. Prepare useful proposals when outside saved scope; do not turn a content calendar into authorization to DM people.

If Instagram is absent, prepare available-handle candidates, searchable display name, truthful concise bio, category, compliant profile image, link destination and tracking plan. Ask the owner to create/connect the chosen account. Continue other verified channels; account metadata cannot guarantee ranking.

## Plan one rolling month, update from evidence

Maintain 30 dated project-local days ahead, replacing consumed days and reviewing the next week after learning. The plan is editable; urgent product fixes and new evidence can displace a weak idea.

Give U.S. and UK buyer problems, examples, channel opportunities and audience-active posting windows first consideration under the default strategy; honor an owner's saved market override. Use platform audience data to refine country and regional time-zone choices, accounting for daylight-saving changes. Keep the owner's run/reporting time zone separate. Maintain globally useful stories and evidenced opportunities elsewhere; priority does not mean geoblocking, excluding other followers or duplicating every post for each country. No arbitrary geographic posting quota or extra posts beyond the shared account allocation are implied.

Every calendar entry contains:

```text
date/time/timezone; project; campaign/content identity
audience problem; angle; funnel stage; claim/source record
destination account IDs; format; asset requirements
CTA and tagged landing URL; success metric and review date
personal-X selection and shared slot if applicable
owner/agent status; package fingerprint; provider operation IDs
```

Use draft entries for later days: a supported buyer problem, proposed angle, missing evidence and preparation date are enough to plan work. Leave unverified fields explicitly pending. Fingerprints and provider IDs appear only after their actual preparation/submission stages; a planning entry is not an accepted schedule. If a date, destination or useful angle cannot yet be established, record that gap and continue ready work rather than inventing a complete calendar.

Default to one useful main brand story daily, adapted for enabled relevant channels and their saved cadence. The two daily blog candidates can supply source material; they do not require two identical promotional posts on every platform. Adapt cadence from account data, audience fit and platform rules.

Never schedule a claim that will become unverified before release. Recheck queued comparisons, price/limit announcements and time-bound availability before their publishing window. Amend or cancel only the affected pending item through a recorded provider action.

## Choose channels from buyer evidence

Use the buyer segments and regional purchase-readiness findings in [Customer and revenue decisions](customer-revenue.md). Maintain a small ranked discovery/test queue alongside the calendar; a connected account is a usable destination, not evidence that its audience will buy.

Record each opportunity as:

```text
channel/surface; buyer segment and region; discovery/evaluation behavior
evidence/source/date; useful contribution and route to first value/paid use
production/maintenance effort; included allowance; confidence and priority
exact destination; authorization status; bounded test and review/stop rule
```

Consider relevant search surfaces, existing integration ecosystems, reputable directories and buyer communities as evidence warrants. Rank audience fit, purchase intent and likely paid value against effort; do not prefer broad reach automatically. Continue suitable daily brand publishing while testing a bounded opportunity. Reuse prepared assets when useful, with current claims and destination-specific review.

Execute through mapped destinations within saved authority. For an ungranted third-party submission, new account, partnership contact or direct outreach, prepare the concrete package and record the missing scope; discovery does not authorize submission or messaging. Use existing included tools and allowances only.

## Useful customer-initiated discovery

When actual use supports it, assess optional sharing of useful outputs, reusable templates, public worked examples or discovery through an existing integration. A sharing path should help the customer complete their task and give a relevant new buyer a truthful route to the product; do not add a referral feature merely to fill the calendar.

Keep sharing deliberately customer-initiated, with clear control over the exact public content and destination. Do not expose private data, access address books, automatically send invitations or introduce financial rewards. Test authorized reversible product changes through the normal checks; third-party publication and integration permissions still apply. Measure qualified referrals, activation and paid outcomes where attribution permits.

## Shared founder-account calendar

Before reserving personal X, read the one shared founder calendar used by all enrolled projects. Enforce its total daily allocation, spacing and existing reservations, including posts created outside this project when visible.

Reserve a slot atomically using project/content/account identity. Recheck availability before submission. If shared state cannot be read or reserved reliably, defer personal amplification and continue brand work. Never let each project independently assume it owns the same slot.

Choose amplification by audience usefulness, evidence and project priority, not equal airtime or blanket reposting. Use a fresh hook and founder-supported context. Do not manufacture first-person claims or quote a hypothetical customer as real.

## Visual and text acceptance rules

Apply the saved content policy to every image, carousel slide, video frame, thumbnail, subtitle, caption, voice script, thread reply and link-preview asset. During onboarding, present the configurable `modest_music_free` preset and record any explicit owner override. This preset starts with:

- No visible women, nudity, sexualized/intimate imagery, alcohol, gambling, pork, drugs, profanity, blasphemous expletives or other recorded owner-prohibited wording.
- Preserve additional person/source likeness exclusions recorded by the current owner. Keep private review references local unless their separate use is authorized.
- Inspect people and embedded screenshots, avatars, browser chrome and background material. If identity or permissibility is uncertain, replace with clearly acceptable factual product imagery.
- Do not remove context in a way that creates a false claim. Replace unsuitable examples rather than merely hiding a word.
- Prefer actual product screenshots, typography, diagrams, cursor demonstrations and useful templates. Avoid irrelevant decorative media.
- Use licensed/owned assets and preserve necessary attribution. Never fabricate endorsement, user proof or shipped UI. Remove private information and removable metadata before upload.

Review each final asset at full size and destination preview size. Check spelling, numbers, links, visual policy, crop-safe text, legibility, captions, logo accuracy and claim alignment. A prompt containing restrictions is not a completed review.

## Video generation and audio review

Under `modest_music_free`, narration is allowed, **music is prohibited and sound effects default to off**. Honor explicit saved owner overrides without silently weakening the configured policy. Apply that policy to intros, transitions, template tracks, background source audio and the exported file. The steps below implement the default music-free workflow; inspect the current companion skill or renderer's documentation before using its options.

1. Discover a relevant installed video skill and inspect its current audio defaults. Choose `brag`, a HyperFrames workflow, Remotion or another verified capability for the actual task; stay within existing allowances.
2. For an installed `brag` version that supports them, explicitly pass **`--no-music --no-sfx`** and repeat those requirements in the storyboard/composition brief. Verify current options; do not assume defaults disable audio or invent unsupported flags.
3. If an installed `product-launch-video` workflow supports storyboard YAML **`music: none`**, set it explicitly. Follow that version's silent-export contract when a fully silent asset is intended; skip narration and SFX and remove stale audio metadata through its supported workflow.
4. For narrated `product-launch-video`, retain the approved script, explicitly disable BGM and SFX in the chosen current workflow, and use an allowed existing voice/provider. Do not invoke a default music/SFX fetch step. If narration cannot be separated reliably from music, choose a different workflow or a silent export.
5. Treat existing source video audio as unreviewed. Replace or remove prohibited music; preserve speech only when the final track is compliant and meaning remains truthful.
6. Inspect the final rendered video's audio through listening/media review, including transitions and the start/end. Stream metadata or a "music: none" setting alone cannot prove compliance. A fully silent export should also have no unintended audible track.
7. Review the exact final frames/cover/captions and audio, bind the review to the exported file hash, and invalidate it after any media or packaging change. If reliable review is unavailable, hold that video and use an already reviewed image/text package.

Honor companion skills' technical checks and render conventions. The owner's saved scope resolves ordinary creative checkpoints; do not reopen the same interview for every daily asset.

## Package and guarded delivery

Create native copy per destination and one manifest containing project/content identity, exact account IDs, planned time/timezone, claim record, links, final media hashes, review evidence and destination-specific fields. Preserve originals; derivatives receive separate hashes and reviews.

Before submission:

1. Verify standing scope, exact account identity, current provider constraints and available allowance.
2. Validate copy, claims, content policy, final media/audio, links and the reserved calendar slot.
3. Check local ledger and remote state for duplicates. Reserve the operation before the external call using an atomic lock/transaction or verified provider idempotency feature.
4. Send only the reviewed package through the configured supported adapter. Keep its request/operation ID and destination results without exposing credentials.

Keep `prepared`, `scheduled`, `published`, `verified`, `failed`, `blocked` and `unknown` distinct. A queue response establishes scheduling only when the provider confirms it; it does not prove publication. Mark published from provider evidence and verified only after checking the public destination/URL. If public verification is unavailable, retain the supported provider state and report verification pending with the limitation.

If a request times out or returns an ambiguous result, record `unknown`, keep the reservation and reconcile provider IDs, exact account, content fingerprint, scheduled time and public feed before retrying. Negative scans may not rule out delayed delivery; keep uncertainty visible. Never immediately replay the full request.

Track each destination independently. Retain successful posts and reconcile/retry only missing destinations when safe. Do not automatically duplicate, delete or replace a successful published post. A proposed correction needs its own scoped recorded action.

## Learning and reporting

Collect available platform metrics once per local day with timestamps. Compare posts at similar ages (for example, 24 hours and seven days); normalize by reach only when available. Do not average incompatible percentage fields or infer registrations from views.

Weekly, connect campaign/content to qualified visits, activation and paying customers where attribution permits. Inspect retention/completion and saves as diagnostics. Record sample size, observation window and uncertainty; do not claim a post caused revenue without sufficient evidence.

Compare mature channel/content cohorts and repeated use of the same asset against known creation, adaptation and maintenance effort. Include retained value where available; small samples and attribution gaps remain uncertainty. Keep useful themes, improve promising paths and stop weak experiments after their agreed window; daily publishing does not require repeating an ineffective angle or using every connected channel.

Change one meaningful creative/distribution hypothesis at a time when feasible: hook, proof, format, CTA or landing path. Preserve the baseline, test window and keep/change/stop decision. Favor useful acquisition over follower counts.

Report verified URLs, scheduled items, unknown/failed destinations, actual creative output, meaningful findings and access repairs. Stay quiet for unchanged non-actionable state. Local generation requires the PC/app/runtime; provider-accepted schedules can execute independently, subject to provider availability.
